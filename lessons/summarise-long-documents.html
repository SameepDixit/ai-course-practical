Summarise a long document without losing the key points

Lesson 5 of 55 min read1 task · 3 questions

Read 20 pages in 3 minutes, then check the parts that matter.

Done

AThe idea

For long files, use a tool that works from your own document. Google's Gemini Notebook (it used to be called NotebookLM) has a free plan. You upload a PDF or paste text, and it answers only from that source. It also shows which part of the source each answer came from.

Whatever tool you use, ask for the same four things: the main point, what I must do, deadlines or numbers, and what is unclear.

Do not upload confidential files to a free tool unless your company allows it. When in doubt, use a sample or ask your IT team.

BYour task

Summarise a leave policy in 4 lines

This sample policy is short so you can test the prompt. With a real document, upload it to Gemini Notebook and use the same prompt.

Your slip: copy-paste prompt 
Summarise the policy below for an employee.

Output exactly:
1. Main point (1 line)
2. What I must do (up to 3 bullets)
3. Dates and numbers (bullets)
4. What is unclear (bullets; write "Nothing" if clear)

Policy text:
"Employees get 18 days of paid leave each year. Leave must be requested at least 3 working days before the start date, except for emergencies. Unused leave, up to 6 days, can be carried over to the next year. Leave for more than 5 days in a row needs the manager's written approval. The leave year runs from 1 April."

Into the press

What you should get

A real example of a good result. Your wording will differ a little. The shape and the facts should match.

Printed resultExample

1. Main point: You get 18 paid leave days a year, and you must request them in advance.
2. What I must do:
- Request leave at least 3 working days before it starts (emergencies are an exception).
- Get written manager approval for more than 5 days in a row.
- Use or carry over unused days before the year ends.
3. Dates and numbers:
- 18 days of paid leave per year
- 3 working days notice
- Carry over up to 6 days
- Leave year starts 1 April
4. What is unclear:
- What counts as an emergency is not defined.

Tap the sheet to print it all at once.

Why it works: The fixed four-part output works for any long document. The "unclear" part is the most useful. It shows what to ask HR or your manager.
