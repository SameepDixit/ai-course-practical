Learn from your reviews

Lesson 5 of 55 min read1 task · 3 questions

Paste your reviews. See what customers keep saying. Fix one thing this week.

Done

AThe idea

One review is one opinion. Ten reviews show a pattern. If four people mention long waiting, that is the first thing to fix.

Copy 10 to 20 recent reviews from Google or from WhatsApp messages into the chatbot. Remove customer names and phone numbers first. Ask for themes: what people like, what they complain about and which reviews mention each one.

Check the result yourself. The AI can miscount or put a review in the wrong group. Read the reviews in each group and see if they really say the same thing.

Reply to a bad review in calm words. Thank them, say sorry for the exact problem, say what you will change and invite them back. Change the reply so it says only what you will really do. Do not argue, and do not offer a discount for removing the review.

BYour task

Find the themes in a salon's reviews and reply to a bad one

Use these 10 short reviews for a salon in Lucknow. Next time, paste your own reviews, without names.

Your slip: copy-paste prompt 
Role: You help a small salon owner understand customer reviews.
Task: 1) Group these reviews into themes, with review numbers. 2) Give the top 2 things to fix. 3) Write a reply to review 4.
Reviews:
1. Good haircut, but I waited 40 minutes.
2. Very clean salon, friendly staff.
3. Great facial. Fair price.
4. Booked for 5 pm, served at 6 pm. Nobody told me. Not coming back.
5. Polite staff. Long wait on Sundays.
6. Nice threading, quick service.
7. AC not working, very hot inside.
8. Clean place, good hair colour.
9. Long wait even with a booking.
10. Friendly staff, fair price.
Format: Reply under 60 words, polite, no excuses. Simple English.

Into the press

What you should get

A real example of a good result. Your wording will differ a little. The shape and the facts should match.

TOP 2 THINGS TO FIX
1. Waiting time. Keep to booking times, and message customers when you are running late.

REPLY TO REVIEW 4
