Plan a week of content in 10 minutes

Lesson 5 of 55 min read1 task · 3 questions

One topic, five angles, one clear plan you can follow.

Done

AThe idea

Posting daily is hard when you start from a blank page every morning. Plan once, post all week. Pick one audience and one theme. Then turn that theme into five angles: a tip, a mistake, a story, a tool and a question.

Ask for a table. For each day you need three things: the idea, the format (text, image, short video) and the first line (the hook).

Keep it small. Five posts you really make beat thirty you never finish. Write them in one sitting, then schedule them using your phone's free tools.

BYour task

Make a 5-day plan for freshers starting their first job

Paste the prompt. Replace the audience with yours later.

Your slip: copy-paste prompt 
Make a 5-day content plan (Monday to Friday) for people in their first year at an office job in India.

Theme: "Small habits that make you look reliable".
Use these five angles, one per day: a tip, a common mistake, a short story, a free tool, a question for the audience.

Output a table with columns: Day, Angle, Format (text, image or short video), Idea in one line, First line (hook, under 12 words).
No hashtags. No emojis. Do not mention any company.

What you should get

Why it works: The five fixed angles stop the plan from becoming five copies of the same tip. Hooks under 12 words are easy to test.
